# AUDIENCE.md — who this site is for

Filled from soundplexstudios.com (2026-09-02) and the venue's positioning. Items marked
**[assumed]** are inferences to validate with the client — see "Still unvalidated."

## The shift, stated plainly

Everyone is a creator now — podcasts, music, video, live shows — but home setups hit a quality
ceiling fast, and creating alone is isolating. At the same time, people are starved for third
places: rooms where something is actually happening. SoundPlex rides both shifts at once — a
pro-grade production facility that doubles as a live venue and creative community ("more than a
studio — a movement"), in a South Jersey market with no equivalent under one roof.

## The primary reader

A South Jersey / Philly-area creator in their late 20s–50s: a podcaster who's outgrown the
closet-and-USB-mic setup, a musician who needs a real room and a real engineer, or a business
professional who wants their content to look and sound credible. They're within ~30 minutes of
Pennsauken (Cherry Hill, Moorestown, Merchantville, Camden County), searching things like
"podcast studio near me." Secondary primary: the *audience member* — a local who wants great
small-room shows, screenings and workshops without going into Philly.

## Their pains, in their own words

- "My recordings sound amateur next to everyone else's."
- "I have the ideas but not the gear — and I'm not buying $10k of equipment."
- "Recording at home, there's always a dog barking or a kid yelling."
- "I don't know any other creators around here — I'm doing this completely alone."
- "Every studio I call is booked, sketchy, or charges like it's LA." **[assumed]**
- "There's never anything interesting to do around here that isn't a bar."

## Trigger events

- Just committed to launching a podcast (or season 2, done right this time).
- Band booked a release / needs a demo that doesn't sound like a basement.
- A business owner decided this is the year for video/content marketing.
- Heard an event is happening at SoundPlex → wants to know what this place is.
- New to the area, looking for a creative scene to plug into.

## Objections — and how the brand answers

| Objection | The honest answer |
|---|---|
| "I can just record at home for free." | You can — and it'll sound like it. Pro rooms, engineers and gear are the difference audiences hear. (An entire post exists on this: studio vs. home setup.) |
| "Studios are expensive." | Hourly rentals and memberships from $19/mo — you rent the ceiling-height quality only when you need it. |
| "Memberships are a gym-membership trap." | Three tiers matched to how you actually show up: audience ($19), superfan ($59), working creator ($199). Each lists exactly what you get. |
| "I don't need a community, just a room." | Book the room, then. But the built-in audience and promotion is why members' work gets heard. |
| "Where even is Pennsauken?" | 6713 Rudderow Ave — minutes from Cherry Hill and Merchantville, with easy parking (on-site, street, and two lots nearby). |

## Voice — do / don't

**Do:** energetic but plainspoken; "create, connect, perform"; talk about what happens IN the rooms
(shows, sessions, conversations); invite ("you belong here"); concrete perks and prices.

**Don't:** audio-nerd jargon walls (preamp model numbers as identity); corporate event-space speak
("versatile solutions"); hype without a date, price or room attached; pretending to be an LA/NYC
flagship — the warmth and locality IS the brand.

## Secondary segments

- **Event attendees / fans** — served by Studio Productions listings + Audience membership.
- **Photographers / videographers** — the rooms rent for shoots.
- **Local businesses** — content production + the Plex Collective networking concept.
- **Touring/regional acts needing a small room** **[assumed]** — served by a rooms/booking page.

## Explicitly NOT the target

Major-label projects shopping for a big-desk flagship; national podcast networks; anyone comparing
against Philadelphia's largest venues. SoundPlex wins on access, community and proximity, not scale.
