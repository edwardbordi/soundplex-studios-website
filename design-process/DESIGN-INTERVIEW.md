# Design interview

Ask these when designing a new site. The answers become the **design brief** (`DESIGN-BRIEF.md`), which
drives the theme (`globals.css`) and the components. The `site-design-interview` skill runs this
conversationally and writes the brief for you.

Keep answers concrete. "Modern and clean" is not an answer; "high-contrast, lots of white space, one
sharp accent, no rounded corners" is.

## Brand & feeling

1. In three words, how should the site *feel*? (e.g. calm, premium, bold, technical, warm)
2. Three brands/sites whose look you admire — and one word for *why* each.
3. Anything to avoid? (colors, styles, clichés, competitors' looks)
4. Who is this for, and what should they feel in the first 5 seconds?

## Color

5. Do you have brand colors (hex)? If not, a color you're drawn to?
6. Light, dark, or both?
7. One accent or a small palette? What should the accent be used for (buttons, links, highlights)?

## Type

8. Any brand fonts? Otherwise: serif, sans, or mono for headings? For body?
9. Big and dramatic type, or restrained and readable?

## Layout & density

10. Spacious and airy, or dense and information-rich?
11. Corners: sharp, slightly rounded, or pill/very round?
12. How much motion/animation — none, subtle, or expressive?
13. Full-width and immersive, or contained/columnar?

## Voice & copy

14. How should the writing sound? (plain, expert, playful, formal…)
15. First person ("I/we") or third person?
16. Any words/phrases you always use — or never use?

## Imagery

17. Photography, illustration, abstract/graphic, or minimal/none?
18. Do you have a logo + brand assets, or do we need placeholders?

## Practical

19. Must-have sections on the home page?
20. Any accessibility or performance requirements beyond the defaults?
