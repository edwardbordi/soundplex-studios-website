Walk in and the room decides the mood for you. Fifteen-foot ceilings, the brick wall washed magenta, art on every surface, and a stage that puts the first row close enough to read the set list. Bands play it, comics work it, founders give talks from it, and some nights it’s a wedding. Eighty people fit without anyone feeling like they’re in the back.
