This room has heard things. It was Victor Records’ Studio B, and the tin ceiling that was restored rather than replaced is the one the old sessions were cut under. Today it’s where SoundPlex records, and where the smaller shows happen — seats in a circle, a grand piano, no bad seat because there is no back row.
