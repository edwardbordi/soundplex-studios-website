Before becoming Studio A, this space served as Studio B for Victor Records during the company’s time as a SoundPlex tenant. Its restored tin ceiling remains part of the room’s character, while double acoustic walls help keep outside sounds out.

Today, Studio A is equipped for recording, performance, and film. It features a grand piano, Hammond B3 organ with Leslie speaker, Ludwig drums, percussion instruments, and a vocal isolation booth. The room also hosts our Cinema Salon, where you can screen your film or video project with your crew and connect with SoundPlex’s production team to help take it further.
