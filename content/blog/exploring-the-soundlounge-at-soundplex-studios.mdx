## The SoundLounge: A Fusion of History and Modernity

The walls of the [SoundLounge at SoundPlex Studios](/rooms/sound-lounge) tell tales of musical legends. Once the former offices of Dick Clark and the Victor Talking Machine Co., this space has been masterfully transformed while preserving its historical essence. The lounge boasts a majestic, 14-foot high, original restored tin ceiling and original hardwood flooring, transporting visitors back to the roaring 1920s. The speakeasy vibe is palpable, offering a unique contrast to the modern recording studio facilities it houses.

## Amenities and Features of the SoundLounge

Spanning an impressive 1800 Sq. Ft with a capacity to comfortably host 60 people, the SoundLounge is an ideal choice for a recording studio rental. Its practical amenities include a kitchen, shower, and locker space, along with a private lounge and admin area tailored for studio requirements. The highlight, however, is its creative soul: a chic oak piano bar featuring a Baldwin® upright acoustic piano, set against the backdrop of vibe lighting. The lounge's chic and comfortable seating areas, coupled with two strategically mounted LED TV screens, make it perfect for both casual gatherings and professional meetings.
