In the heart of New Jersey, SoundPlex Studios opens its doors to musicians, artists, and creative professionals seeking quality space for their projects. Our studio rentals cater to a wide range of needs, from recording music to hosting events, all within a building steeped in musical history.

## Our Studios

At the core of SoundPlex Studios is Studio B, our premier recording space. It's a room where sound comes alive, thanks to its size and acoustic design. We've equipped Studio B with top-tier gear to ensure your recordings meet professional standards. The studio boasts a high-end mixing console, industry-standard recording software like Logic Pro X and Pro Tools, and an impressive collection of microphones. The sound processing equipment and monitor speakers complete the package, giving you all the tools you need to bring your musical vision to life.

But Studio B is just the beginning. The SoundLounge offers a versatile space for smaller performances or extended recording sessions. With its own kitchen and shower facilities, it's perfect for those long, creative days when you're in the zone and don't want to leave. For visual artists or those looking to host events, our GalleryRoom provides an elegant backdrop. Its walls showcase work from local artists, adding inspiration to any gathering.

When it comes to live performances, the WoodRoom stands out. Its rustic charm and spacious layout make it ideal for bands looking to rehearse or perform. And for those seeking our newest offering, SoundPlex West provides a great space for band practices and photo shoots alike.

## What Sets Us Apart

At SoundPlex Studios, we understand that great spaces are just the start. That's why we offer flexible rental options to suit your needs, whether you're looking to book by the hour or set up a longer-term arrangement. For our regular visitors, we've created membership options that provide added value and convenience.

Our experienced staff is always on hand to offer advice and support. We believe in fostering a community of artists, and our studios often become a place where creative minds meet and collaborations are born. To further support your creative process, we provide a range of instruments for use, including quality pianos and drum kits.

## A Space for Everyone

Whether you're a solo artist fine-tuning your sound, a band gearing up for your next gig, a podcaster seeking crystal-clear audio, or a business looking for a unique event venue, SoundPlex Studios has a space for you. Our diverse range of studios means we can accommodate various project types and group sizes.

## Rich in History

When you step into SoundPlex Studios, you're not just entering a modern creative space – you're becoming part of a legacy. Our building once housed parts of the Victor Talking Machine Co., and even served as office space for music industry legend Dick Clark. This rich history infuses our studios with a unique atmosphere that many find inspiring.
