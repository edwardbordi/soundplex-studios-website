## A subheading looks like this

Two hashes and a space. Use these to break the post into sections — most people
scan before they read, and subheadings are what they scan.

You can use **bold for emphasis**, and lists:

- like this
- and this

And links, like [this one to the about page](/about).

## Linking to other pages

For pages on this site, use a short path: `/about`, `/blog/some-post`. For
other websites, paste the full address starting with https:// — the site adds
an outbound arrow automatically.

## Two rules that aren't optional

**Every statistic needs a link to where it came from**, and the sentence has to
say what the source actually says. If a study found 23 percent, write 23
percent — not "about a quarter", not "nearly a third".

**Nothing invented.** No made-up numbers, no results that didn't happen.
Describing a plan is fine. Claiming a result isn't.

## Ending

Finish with something the reader can do this week. Specific and small beats a
summary of what they just read.
