## This is your example post

Every post in `content/blog/*.mdx` must satisfy the frontmatter contract in
`src/lib/blog/schema.ts`. The schema is `.strict()` and validated at build time, so a missing or
misspelled field **fails the build** — that's the point. You can't ship an SEO-incomplete post.

## How to write a new post

1. Copy this file to `content/blog/<your-slug>.mdx` (filename must equal the `slug`).
2. Fill in every frontmatter field — a unique `title`, `description` (~155 chars), `excerpt`, exactly
   three `tags`, a `heroImage` in `/public/blog/`, its `heroImageAlt`, and a single `focusKeyword`.
3. Write genuinely useful, first-hand content — cite real sources and give real examples. Unique
   content is what wins in Google **and** AI search; templated/thin content does not.
4. The post auto-appears in the blog index, the sitemap, the RSS feed, and gets its canonical +
   OpenGraph + `BlogPosting` JSON-LD — all from the frontmatter. No page edits needed.

Then delete this example post.
