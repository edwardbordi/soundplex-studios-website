The SoundPlex® organization is pleased to announce a brand new partnership with 102.9 WMGK that will see SoundPlex's world renowned recording studio become the sole official sponsor of 102.9 WMGK's House Band Competition! In the Philadelphia radio market, 102.9 WMGK has long been a high water mark of all that is best in music, and the station has long supported local artists. In this manner, The SoundPlex's goals align precisely with that of 102.9 WMGK, and this partnership will serve to benefit the region's music industry. The SoundPlex® will work with the winners of the contest to create several songs in the recording studio – along with filming the process utilizing The SoundPlex® A/V team.
