There are plenty of reasons to choose SoundPlex® Studios for your next recording project…but here are our top 10!

## 1. The Gear

We've got it all! From a real Hammond B-3 with Leslie rotating speaker – to Neve 1073s channel strips, Neumann and RCA-Victor ribbon microphones, pristine AD/DA, boutique Universal Audio®, Neve®, and Focusrite® tube and solid state compressor/limiters, a brand new Mara Machines 1" 8 track tape recorder, drum kits, world-class monitoring systems for playback and recording, a beautiful ebony baby grand 88 key piano – and MUCH MUCH more.

## 2. The Engineer

Randy Weaver is a GENIUS of recorded sound. With over 40 years experience, you're in safe hands while Randy is behind the mixing console – you'll never be disappointed in the result…**and** he's one of the industry's nicest humans.

## 3. The Sound

Looking for a BIG studio sound – no better place to look than a BIG studio. Looking for a tight sound? We do that too! Consult with us on the sound you're looking for, and we've got the experience, space, and ability to bring your recorded sound dreams to life.

![The control room at SoundPlex Studios](/blog/10-reasons-to-choose-soundplex-studios-control-room.webp)

## 4. The Facilities

In short, we're massive! We've got parking for 50, private lounges while you wait for recording tasks (or other musicians to finish overdubs or mixing), full kitchen facilities, showers, enough space for TOUR BUSES and much much much more. Check out our [rooms](/rooms)!

## 5. The History

It started life as a factory, became Dick Clark's New Jersey area offices during his time hosting 'American Bandstand' in Philadelphia, it was an assembly, administration, and recording studio for the famous VICTOR RECORDS – and now…it's the incredible community performing arts staple known as The SoundPlex®. We're just getting started!

## 6. The Value

You won't find better value! SoundPlex® Studios delivers the best value for a major label caliber professional recording facility in the entire music industry….no joke! We love music and we want to make it with YOU.

![Studio B at SoundPlex Studios](/blog/10-reasons-to-choose-soundplex-studios-studio-b.webp)

## 7. The Vibe

Don't let the largeness fool you! We GET vibe. Incredible works of art, comfortable and chic seating for guests, high ceilings, and some very groovy murals and lighting make this space an artist's HAVEN in which to make any kind of music.

## 8. The Size

SoundPlex® Studio was originally built to be the largest live room on the east coast – and it meets those standards today! The room is capable of fitting a full size 100 piece orchestra or a solo artist…all with the same level of intimacy and professionalism.

## 9. The Location

We are in the cradle of the music industry, Camden County, NJ – just 10 minutes from the beautiful city of Philadelphia (and less than 90 minutes from New York City). We are located in an easily accessed, green, peaceful mixed residential area. You'll fall in love!

## 10. The Support

We're here for you! We don't want our relationship with our artists to end at recording….we know how hard it is to get your music 'out there'…and we want to help! If you need help promoting and marketing your recording…. talk to us! We can help!
