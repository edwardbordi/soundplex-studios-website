## What Is a Podcast Studio Rental?

A podcast studio rental is a professionally built recording space that you book by the hour or through a membership, with the microphones, cameras, acoustics, and engineering support already in place. Instead of buying thousands of dollars in equipment and treating a room in your home, you walk in, sit down, and record. Everything from mic technique to multi-camera video is handled by gear and people who do this every day.

## How Much Does a Podcast Studio Rental Cost?

Podcast studio rental pricing generally falls into three tiers:

**Hourly rentals.** Most professional podcast studios charge by the hour, and rates vary widely by market. In major cities like New York or Los Angeles, expect $150 to $400 or more per hour for a video-equipped studio. In the Philadelphia and South Jersey area, rates are friendlier. Our podcasting studios at SoundPlex rent for $250 per hour with the full video and audio setup included, and we cover [current availability and rates](/blog/local-podcast-studio-insights-availability-rates-and-what-to-expect) in more detail in our local studio insights guide.

**Memberships.** If you record on a schedule, a membership can beat straight hourly pricing. Our [Center Stage membership](/membership) is $199 per month and includes one 60-minute podcast session each month, which on its own is worth more than the membership price, plus dedicated scheduling for premium time slots and promotional support through our media team and influencer network.

**Production add-ons.** Editing, mixing, videography, and marketing support are often billed separately. Ask what is bundled before you book. At SoundPlex, professional sound engineering and videography services are available.

Compare that to the do-it-yourself route. A credible home video podcast setup runs $2,000 to $5,000 once you buy quality microphones, cameras, lighting, an interface, and acoustic treatment, and it still will not sound like a treated room. Searching "podcast studio rental near me" will surface options, but before you book, check for these five things:

1. **Video capability included in the base rate**, not as an upsell.
2. **A real acoustic environment**, not an office with foam panels.
3. **Guest capacity** that matches your format, including room for a producer.
4. **Engineering and videography services** available if you want a hands-off session.
