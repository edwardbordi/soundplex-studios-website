These vintage photos of The SoundPlex® are mind blowing! Share these on your social media and be sure to tag us if you have any memories of our building!

![A train at the loading dock of what is now the WoodRoom, when the building was the Perkins Ice and Dairy Processing Facility in 1915](/blog/5-awesome-vintage-photos-of-the-soundplex-1.webp)

***1. Taken from the loading dock of what is now 'The WoodRoom', The SoundPlex® was operating in 1915 as Perkins Ice & Dairy Processing Facility!***

![The building in 1949 as the Tru-ade Bottling Co. of Camden, NJ](/blog/5-awesome-vintage-photos-of-the-soundplex-2.webp)

***2. In 1949, The SoundPlex® was occupied by the 'Tru-ade Bottling Co', of Camden, NJ!***

![SoundPlex Studio under construction and restoration in 2011](/blog/5-awesome-vintage-photos-of-the-soundplex-3.webp)

***3. This photo from 2011 shows SoundPlex® Studio still being built and restored!!***

![A 1900 photograph of the original foundation stone at 249 Rudderow Ave, now 6713 Rudderow](/blog/5-awesome-vintage-photos-of-the-soundplex-4.webp)

***4. This photo shows the original foundation upon which The SoundPlex® is built at 6713 Rudderow (then known as 249 Rudderow Ave.) and the driveway space that it shares with 6701 Rudderow Ave today. This 1900 photo shows the foundation stone which was later bricked and built upon to create the brick structure we all know (and love) today as 'The SoundPlex®'. This bedrock is still the basis of most of the property while 'The WoodRoom' (which would be to the left of the taker of this photo) is the oldest portion of the building from the form that it knew in this photo in 1900.***

![An illustration of MACK delivery trucks used by the E.S. Perkins Co. to deliver milk and ice in Camden County](/blog/5-awesome-vintage-photos-of-the-soundplex-5.webp)

***5. Not a photo, but a wonderful illustration of the MACK® delivery trucks utilized by The E.S. Perkins Co. for delivery of milk and ice to the Camden County area. This was drawn from the same perspective as seen in photo 1!***
