## Not just another boutique

Step into style with Lucille's Loft, the newest creative member of the SoundPlex Studios family! This isn't just another boutique, it's a curated fashion experience where pre-loved meets high-end, and every piece tells a story.

- Doors open at 5 PM
- Tickets: $20
- Includes a $20 credit toward your first purchase!

Enjoy light fare and wine, browse through hand-picked boutique clothing at a fraction of the price, and welcome Lucille as she unveils her fresh take on fashion with purpose. Expect a cozy, stylish atmosphere in the SoundPlex Loft, surrounded by art, creativity, and good company.

Plus: a few special guest vendors will be joining us to round out your shopping experience with unique finds.

Shop. Sip. Socialize. Come celebrate the art of resale, and leave with a story in your bag.
