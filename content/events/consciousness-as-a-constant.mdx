## Consciousness As a Constant

What if consciousness isn't simply something we possess, but something fundamental to the universe itself?

From a very young age, I have always been curious about how things work, including the one process we experience every moment of every day but rarely stop to consider: consciousness.

Where does it come from? What does it truly mean to be conscious? Is consciousness created by the brain, or could it be a constant woven into the very fabric of existence?

This September, SoundPlex Studios introduces a thought-provoking new podcast: Consciousness As a Constant.

I will share a profound personal experience that changed the way I view consciousness, life and our place in the universe. I'll also engage in open, honest conversations with medical practitioners, philosophers, researchers and spiritual thinkers, exploring different perspectives without pretending that any one of us has all the answers.

Together, we'll ask some of humanity's greatest questions: Who are we? Why are we here? What are we becoming, and where are we going?

Stay tuned for a fascinating, frank and deeply personal exploration of the greatest mystery we all share. Consciousness As a Constant, premiering this September at SoundPlex Studios, before a live studio audience.
