## Psychedelic Parlor – Exploring Healing and Consciousness Expansion

Join us for another Psychedelic Parlor, hosted by Denise Brough of TooMushLove at SoundPlex Studios.

As psychedelic-assisted therapies and plant medicines become part of mainstream conversations, it's more important than ever to replace stigma and misinformation with thoughtful education, honest questions, and open community dialogue.

This gathering offers a welcoming space to explore their potential benefits, the importance of professional guidance, harm reduction, safety, and responsible use. Whether you're curious, experienced, or simply interested in learning more, you're invited to be part of the conversation.

Real understanding begins when we gather, listen, and learn from one another.

Free | Limited seating | Sober-only event

Step inside the artful, industrial-chic atmosphere of SoundPlex Studios for our next Mushroom Magic Meetup, a community conversation on the therapeutic, emotional, and spiritual power of psilocybin and plant medicine.

Whether you're curious, cautious, or already on the journey, this welcoming, educational event is designed to inform, connect, and empower. Hosted in our intimate lounge space, you'll learn in a grounded, confidential, and inspiring setting built for authentic dialogue.

**Topics we'll explore:**

- The science behind psilocybin, mental health and longevity
- Microdosing vs. macrodosing: what's the difference?
- Psychedelic integration and emotional processing
- Personal stories of healing and transformation

Includes Q&A and an optional community sharing circle.

This is a sober, inclusive space designed for education, not consumption. Expand your understanding. Reconnect with your self. Reimagine what's possible.

RSVP now, seating is limited!
