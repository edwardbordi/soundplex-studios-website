## Sip & Shop at Lucille's Loft

Update: Santa has spoken... free admission for all! Sunday, December 7th, 11 AM–5 PM at Lucille's Loft / SoundPlex Studios.

Christmas magic is alive and well at SoundPlex Studios! After hearing from many of you, Santa himself whispered in our ear and told us he wants everyone to enjoy this special day of holiday joy, community, and festive shopping, with no cost to enter.

Admission is now free for everyone. For those wonderful guests who already paid the $20 donation, you will receive a $20 shopping coupon at the door to use toward any purchase at the event. Santa insisted.

**Event highlights:**

- Two hours with Santa for photos, smiles, and magic
- Rita's Secret Santa Shop: kids 12 and under get play money to pick a gift for Mom, wrapped for Christmas morning
- Food, drinks and holiday fun throughout the entire day

**Featured vendors:** Gourmet Mama, Bugs Embroidery, Blissimo Handcrafted Gifts, Bling Pens, Love Bugs Sweet Treats, Delane Crystals, Midlife Messy Mystic (tarot readings), and more festive shopping surprises!

Step into a warm, welcoming holiday marketplace where SoundPlex Studios and Lucille's Loft create a magical fusion of fashion, art, community, and Christmas cheer.

Bring your family, bring your friends. Santa wants everyone to enjoy this day!
