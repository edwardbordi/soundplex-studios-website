## The Cinema Salon at SoundPlex Studios presents: The Cafone

Join us for a special premiere screening of Episodes 1 & 2 of The Cafone inside the intimate, cinematic setting of The Cinema Salon at SoundPlex Studios.

This is more than a screening. It's a relaxed evening built around film, food, wine, and conversation, the way independent films are meant to be experienced.

**Guests will enjoy:**

- Wine tastings throughout the evening
- Linguine and clams
- Strawberry Fields salad
- Premiere screenings of The Cafone, Episodes 1 & 2
- An intimate salon-style atmosphere surrounded by fellow film lovers

Settle into the SoundPlex, enjoy a glass of wine and a great meal, and experience The Cafone on the big screen in a setting designed for conversation and connection.

**The Cinema Salon:** intimate films, deeper conversations, unforgettable nights.

Doors 6:00 PM. Limited seating available due to the intimate nature of the event. Come hungry. Come curious. And come ready for a great night of independent film.
