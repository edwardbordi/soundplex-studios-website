## Psychedelic Parlor – Exploring Healing and Consciousness Expansion

Introducing the next chapter from TooMushLove: an educational workshop on the healing power of plant medicines.

TooMushLove was born from a powerful vision: to educate, inspire, and support those seeking deeper healing, transformation, and a return to authenticity. This new workshop offering is not about trends, it's about truth. It's about reconnecting to the wisdom of nature and understanding how plant medicines like psilocybin are opening new doors in the world of mental health and emotional wellbeing.

In a world filled with overstimulation, stress, and emotional overload, TooMushLove creates safe, informed, and grounded spaces to explore the clinical and personal benefits of these ancient tools.

**What to expect:**

- **Evidence-based insight:** Learn about the therapeutic potential of psychedelics like psilocybin, and how research is reshaping treatment approaches for depression, PTSD, anxiety, and addiction.
- **Lived experience:** Gain perspective from Denise's own professional and personal journey, from traditional psychotherapy to psychedelic guiding, illustrating how these tools can catalyze profound transformation and inner clarity.
- **Interactive dialogue:** Participate in an open Q&A and guided discussion designed to deepen your understanding and address your individual questions in a safe, supportive environment.
- **Curated space:** This intimate, sober-only gathering prioritizes confidentiality, connection, and meaningful exchange.

No charge, just RSVP.
