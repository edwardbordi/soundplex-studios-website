## SoundPlex Live – Open Mic

The SoundPlex Open Mic is officially a monthly experience, and it's one you don't want to miss. After a packed house and an incredible debut, our first Open Mic at the WoodRoom Café proved one thing loud and clear: the talent is here, and the room is alive.

Now, we're bringing it back every last Tuesday of the month, and each Open Mic will be professionally filmed in front of a live studio audience, turning every performance into a moment that lives beyond the night.

Step onto a stage designed for artists: grand piano, full drum kit, pro backline, pristine sound, and an audience that's locked in. This isn't just another open mic, it's a chance to be seen, heard, and captured in a space built for real performances.

If you're serious about your craft and ready to play in a room that respects it, this is your monthly home.

- WoodRoom Café at SoundPlex Studios
- Last Tuesday of every month
- Sign-ups at 6:30 PM | Performances 7–9 PM

Come play the room everyone's talking about, and let the cameras roll.
