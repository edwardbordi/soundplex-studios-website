## Gourmet Mama & Gourmet Papa

Episode theme: Gourmet Mama Meets Papa. When dads cook... and Mom makes it fabulous.

Let's be honest. When dads are in a food crunch, the playbook is short: hot dogs, Steak-ums, whatever's in the fridge. In this episode, three dads step into the kitchen, bringing their go-to "emergency meals"... and their opinions.

**Featuring:**

- George Koch, talking breakfast, the flexibility of eggs, and why sometimes a Steak-um just gets the job done
- Ed Bordi, sharing his gluten-free expertise while diving into AI in the kitchen

Then Gourmet Mama steps in. Kelly takes these classic dad shortcuts and dresses them up, transforming quick, no-frills meals into fun, elevated dishes, with a little humor, a little roasting, and a whole lot of flavor. And yes, there may even be a surprise guest.

**Why you'll love this episode:** real, relatable cooking; practical ideas you can actually use; a perfect mix of laughs, food, and creativity. Because at the end of the day, even dad food can shine with the right touch.

Tickets: $35, includes dinner and the live show experience.
