## The premiere of SoundPlex Live, our first open mic

The mic is on. The lights are hot. The moment has come. Be one of the first artists to ever perform inside the brand-new WoodRoom Café, the premier live music space in South Jersey where art, sound, and soul collide.

Hosted by Austin Haines, fellow musician, performer, and energy catalyst, guiding the night with inspiration, rhythm, and connection.

This grand premiere of SoundPlex Open Mic kicks off a new monthly tradition, the last Tuesday of every month, where the region's most passionate musicians, poets, and performers come to be heard, be seen, and be part of something new.

**What's included:**

- Full drum kit
- Grand piano
- Two vocal mics
- Full professional backline
- The signature SoundPlex industrial-chic vibe

Only 20 performer slots available. Act fast, this will fill up quickly.

Performer entry: $10 (includes one non-alcoholic beverage). Audience tickets: same price, same vibe. Come support the launch of something truly special.

Come for the music. Stay for the magic.
