## An unforgettable night of laughter, insight, and old-school comedy gold with Ritch Shydner

"The History of Stand-Up Comedy" with Ritch Shydner, live at SoundPlex Studios. BYOB, lite fare served.

Get ready for an unforgettable night of laughter, insight, and old-school comedy gold with Ritch Shydner, a true stand-up veteran seen on The Tonight Show, Letterman, HBO, Married with Children, and more.

A Grammy-nominated writer for Jeff Foxworthy and Jay Leno, Ritch brings his one-man show "The History of Stand-Up Comedy" to our stage: an insider's journey through the gritty clubs, iconic comics, and behind-the-scenes moments that shaped American comedy.

Limited seating, unmatched vibes, industrial-chic setting. Come early and explore the art walls of SoundPlex.
