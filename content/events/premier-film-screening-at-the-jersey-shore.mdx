## Hop into summer at the premiere film screening of "At The Jersey Shore"

For the first time ever, SoundPlex Studios transforms into a movie house for a special screening of the independent feature film "At The Jersey Shore" by filmmaker Tom Bentley of Suburbanite Productions.

Set against the iconic Jersey Shore backdrop, this film captures the friendships, relationships, and unforgettable moments that only a Jersey summer can bring.

This marks the first feature film screening ever hosted at SoundPlex Studios, a perfect setting where film, music, and art collide.

- Thursday, June 18th
- Doors open: 6:00 PM
- Screening: 7:00 PM

Join us for a great night of independent film and creative community. Limited seating available. BYOB. Food provided by Gourmet Mama. Wine tasting available at no charge.
