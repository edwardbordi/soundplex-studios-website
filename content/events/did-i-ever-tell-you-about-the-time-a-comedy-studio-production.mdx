## "Did I Ever Tell You the Time" has moved to the stunning stages of SoundPlex Studios!

Big news! We're beyond excited to announce that the beloved live show "Did I Ever Tell You the Time" has officially found a new home at the vibrant and artistic SoundPlex Studios!

Hosted by the always entertaining Mark and Maureen, this month's show brings you a night packed with live storytelling, sharp comedy, and audience-driven improv, all set in our eclectic, industrial-chic space.

SoundPlex Studios | BYOB | Lite fare served

Expect an incredible lineup of performers and spontaneous laughs that will leave you inspired and thoroughly entertained. Whether it's your first time or you're a returning fan, this night will be one to remember.

Come early. Grab a seat. Bring your favorite bottle. Let the stories unfold.
