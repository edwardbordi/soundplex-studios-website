## Join us for an unforgettable night of comedy and connections

Buddy's stand-up made him a regular on Comedy Central with appearances on "NickMom Night Out," and he was a contributing writer on "NickMom's Actual Housewives of the PTO." He can be seen opening for Joy Behar and was a frequent opener for Joan Rivers. He has also had the privilege of working with The Beach Boys, Doobie Brothers, and The Righteous Brothers and can be heard on Sirius/XM and Pandora. His Dry Bar Special, "Still in the Driveway," and his CD, "Happy Now?" are out now.
