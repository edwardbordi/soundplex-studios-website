## An evening of sound and serenity at Lucille's Loft

Come early to shop, stay late to shop.

Take a gentle pause for yourself. Join us for an hour of rest, renewal, and deep relaxation through sound and mindfulness on the 2nd floor of Lucille's Loft at SoundPlex Studios.

You'll be guided through a soothing meditation while the vibrations of crystal bowls, chimes, and other healing instruments wash over you, bringing balance to your mind, body, and spirit.

No experience is needed. Just come as you are and let the sound do the work.

**Please bring:** a yoga mat, blanket, open mind, open heart, and water (if desired).

Energy exchange: $30.

Before and after the session, explore the unique atmosphere of Lucille's Loft, a boutique built on love, memory, and mindful fashion.

Space is limited. Reserve your moment of serenity today.
