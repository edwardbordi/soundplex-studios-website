## SoundPlex Originals, live production on February 21st

SoundPlex Studios proudly presents SoundPlex Originals, a live original-music showcase celebrating the artists creating the next wave of sound.

Join us on Saturday, February 21st as the WoodRoom Café at SoundPlex Studios transforms into a discovery space for new music, featuring up to three bands, each delivering 30–45 minute sets of all original material. No covers. No shortcuts. Just real music, performed live.

Tickets: $20, priced to fill the room with true original-music fans, listeners who want to discover, support, and connect with emerging artists.

**For the bands:** bands are limited to 1–5 members.

**Can't make it in person?** This event will be live streamed on our Patreon channel, giving supporters exclusive access to SoundPlex Originals from anywhere.

- WoodRoom Café stage, SoundPlex Studios
- Show: 7:00 PM | Doors: 6:30 PM
- BYOB encouraged
- 100% original music

SoundPlex Originals is about discovery, community, and giving original artists a stage, and an audience, that listens. Come be part of the movement.
