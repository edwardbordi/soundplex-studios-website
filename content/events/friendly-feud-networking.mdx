## Ready to turn a little friendly competition into your next great connection?

Join us at SoundPlex Studios for Friendly Feud, a Family Feud-inspired networking night where business professionals team up, test their answers, and compete for cool prizes!

- Thursday, October 8, 2026
- 6:00–8:00 PM
- WoodRoom Studio at SoundPlex Studios

We're bringing together the energy of a game show and the relaxed atmosphere of our industrial, art-filled WoodRoom. DJ Dougie Fresh will keep the tunes flowing while you enjoy wine and spirit tastings, light fare, and plenty of opportunities to meet new people.

Come solo or bring a colleague, business partner, or friend. Whether you're ready to hit the buzzer or cheer everyone on, you'll be part of the fun.

RSVP to Info@thesoundplex.com and let us know if you're bringing a guest. If you want to compete, please let us know. There is a $10 fee that goes toward prizes.

Bring your competitive spirit. Leave with new connections.
