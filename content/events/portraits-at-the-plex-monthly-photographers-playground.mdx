## Portraits at the Plex – Monthly Photographer's Playground

Calling all photographers, pro or just starting out! Step into one of the most artistic and visually striking spaces around for Portraits at the Plex, a monthly creative gathering hosted at SoundPlex Studios.

On the first Tuesday of every month, from 12 PM to 8 PM, we open our doors to give you full access to our ever-evolving backdrops, stunning lighting setups, and vibrant, music-infused atmosphere. Whether you're shooting a client, a friend, or experimenting with your own creative vision, this is your chance to create something extraordinary.

**What's included:**

- Full access to our themed portrait spaces
- Professional lighting setups
- Refreshments to keep you going
- A vibe that inspires next-level work

Bring your camera, your creativity, and your people, and let's make some magic. No booking necessary, just show up and shoot!

Portraits at the Plex: where vision meets vibe. See you there!
