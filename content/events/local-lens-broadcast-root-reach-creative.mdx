## Creative content with a nostalgic theme

A conversation that leads to something bigger. Some conversations just feel right from the start.

A few weeks ago at SoundPlex Studios, I met Cristina Vann during a private event. We didn't need a long meeting to figure it out: there was an immediate connection around creativity, storytelling, and how people present themselves to the world.

That's exactly what Local Lens is about. It's not just a broadcast, it's a conversation with people who have something real to say and a story worth sharing.

For this Local Lens Broadcast, I'll be sitting down with Cristina, a content creator, connector, and someone who naturally brings energy and authenticity into every room she's in. She has a strong feel for storytelling that connects, branding that feels personal, and creating experiences people remember. And it became pretty clear early on... she just gets it.
