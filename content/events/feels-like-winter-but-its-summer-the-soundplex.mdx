## Summer holiday in January, live at SoundPlex Studios

Beat the winter blues with surf rock and good vibes.

- Doors open: 6:30 PM
- Show starts: 7:00 PM
- BYOB | Bathing suit required! Summer lite fare.

Slide into summer mode for one night only this winter! We're flipping the script and bringing you a mid-winter blast of surf, sun, and sound with a live performance by Blue Wave Theory, the electrifying American surf rock band known for their instrumental grooves and ocean-soaked energy.

But that's not all. We'll kick things off with a very special guest performance by Marques, a uniquely soulful artist making his long-awaited debut. His music is raw, transformative, and deeply rooted in life's real stories.

This is more than just a show, it's a summer state of mind. So bring your own bottle, rock your best beachwear, and let's heat things up at SoundPlex Studios. Filmed before a live studio audience.

Limited seating. Reserve early!
